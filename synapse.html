How it works

Pair once. Then just chat.

Pair

Once

In Hydron on your computer, turn on Phone pairing and press Pair a phone. Type the code it shows into Synapse. The two devices now know each other for good.

Connect

Every time, automatically

Open the app and it finds your computer by itself and reconnects quietly. It remembers exactly which computer it paired with, and refuses to talk to any other machine claiming to be it.

Chat

Whenever you like

Pick any model installed on your PC and talk to it. Replies stream in live, and you can switch models or stop a reply at any point.

Two ways to connect

Works even when your Wi-Fi won't.

Lots of home, office and guest networks deliberately stop devices talking to each other, and PC security software often blocks incoming connections. Most apps just fail there. Synapse switches to Bluetooth and keeps going.

Same network: the phone finds your PC automatically and connects over Wi-Fi.

Privacy

Private by design, in plain words.

Encrypted end to end

Everything between your phone and your computer is encrypted, over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth alike.

Your phone knows your PC

After pairing, the phone remembers your computer's unique identity. If anything else pretends to be it, the phone refuses to connect and tells you — so nobody can sit in the middle and listen, not even while pairing.

Off by default

Until you switch Phone pairing on, your computer isn't listening to the network at all.

A phone can only chat

It can list your installed models and talk to them. It can't run coding agents or commands, download anything, or touch your files. Even a stolen phone can't do more than chat.

Cut off in one tap

Remove a phone instantly from your computer's settings, or unpair from the phone itself.

No account, no cloud

There's no sign-up and no Hydron server. Your chats stay between this phone and your computer.

FAQ

Questions

Does it work when my computer is off?

No. The models run on your PC, so your computer needs to be on with Hydron running. Nothing is stored or processed anywhere else.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no Hydron server. Pairing is done directly between your phone and your computer.

Why won't it connect over Wi-Fi?

Many networks stop devices from talking to each other, and security software on the PC can block incoming connections even when Windows Firewall allows them. Pair your phone and PC in your system's Bluetooth settings and Synapse will use Bluetooth instead.

Can someone with my phone get into my PC?

A phone can only list your models and chat with them. It can't run agents or commands, download anything, or open your files. You can also remove the phone instantly from your computer's settings.

What if someone sees my pairing code?

The code expires after 5 minutes, works only once, and locks after 5 wrong tries. After pairing, your phone only trusts your computer's identity.

Is there an iPhone version?

Synapse is an Android app.

How do I install the APK?

Download the APK on your phone and open it. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file manager the first time; allow it, then tap Install. To check the file, its SHA-256 is 26c354b78fcd41dd1967a015c0187a821ebd7e8499506048da2160e272bec3ed.
